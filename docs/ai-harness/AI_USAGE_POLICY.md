# AI Usage Policy

## AI is optional
The core assessment must remain functional without an external model.

## Permitted AI jobs
- Independent review of a HAP packet.
- Explanation of deterministic findings.
- Identification of contradictions or missing context.
- Suggestion of draft rules for human review.
- Translation or simplification of approved content.

## Prohibited AI jobs
- Autonomous production-rule changes.
- Ungated prescription treatment.
- Fabricated clinical data.
- Silent suppression of red flags.
- Direct mutation of the user's medical history.

## Rule-authoring workflow
AI proposal → draft schema validation → automated tests → source review → clinical review → approval → versioned production rule.
