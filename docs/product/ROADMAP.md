# HealthMap Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Deterministic foundation
- Master-question baseline.
- Adaptive follow-up selection.
- Initial health domains: metabolic, cardiovascular, nutrition, sleep, activity.
- Traceable findings.
- Evidence completeness.
- Red-flag interruption.
- HAP export.

## Phase 2 — Clinical knowledge depth
- Versioned rule registry.
- Broader lab catalogue.
- Condition modules.
- Medication/supplement context.
- Contraindication and special-population gates.
- Test-priority engine.

## Phase 3 — Harnessed AI review
- HEALTH_ASSESSMENT_HARNESS.md injection.
- Strict output-schema validation.
- Independent AI assessment.
- Engine vs AI comparison.
- Deterministic reconciliation/verdict view.

## Phase 4 — Reassessment loop
- Lab entry/import.
- Historical assessments.
- Trends.
- Retest reminders.
- Before/after health map.

## Phase 5 — Rule Studio
- Human-readable rule editor.
- AI draft-rule generation.
- Unit-test generation.
- Version/approval workflow.
- Source/reference registry.

## Phase 6 — Distribution
- API.
- MCP tools.
- ChatGPT app UI.
- Clinician summary export.
- Optional integrations with labs / wearables where appropriate.


## Execution cadence
- Build in 1–2 commits per mission.
- Execute related missions in bounded batches; retain mission-level verification and status.
- Strategic reviews: after M05, after M09, and after M13 before external distribution.
- Medium effort is the default; raise to High for architecture/safety/reconciliation/distribution missions.
